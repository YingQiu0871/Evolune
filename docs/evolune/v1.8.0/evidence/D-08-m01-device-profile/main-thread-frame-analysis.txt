The total-frame histogram includes Activity/test scheduling, dataset swaps and
gesture injection. The draw histogram is the relevant chart-render signal and
is predominantly 5–10 ms; its two >16 ms buckets and zero >33 ms buckets do not
show a meaningful chart-induced jank problem. Gesture wall means are 119.1856
ms for pan and 71.7340 ms for zoom, but include the deliberate 200 ms touch
injection delays and are not presented as user-visible latency.

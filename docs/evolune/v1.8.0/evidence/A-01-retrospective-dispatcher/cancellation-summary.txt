EVOLUNE v1.8.0 A-01 — CANCELLATION/PUBLICATION

The service retains its existing CancellationException rethrow behavior and
does not translate cancellation into unavailable/error. withContext preserves
structured cancellation across the new dispatcher boundary.

Existing service cancellation coverage remains green. The new ViewModel test
cancels the caller scope while the load is gated, releases the gate, and
asserts that the state remains LOADING with null result and null failure. This
preserves the existing rule that cancellation cannot publish a late terminal
success or error.

Generation checks, refresh/coalescing, and stale-result guards remain in their
existing owners and passed the focused and full JVM suites.

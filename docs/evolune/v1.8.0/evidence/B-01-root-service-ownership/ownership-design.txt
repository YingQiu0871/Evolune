EVOLUNE v1.8.0 B-01 — OWNERSHIP DESIGN

Selected solution: Option A, an Activity-owned MainFeatureServices holder.

MainFeatureServices.create(...) is called exactly once from MainActivity.onCreate,
after recoverInterruptedRestoreAtStartup has succeeded and after the existing
repository/backup/onboarding setup, but before setContent. The setContent lambda
captures that one holder and only reads its fields during recomposition.

The holder owns the shared HistoryReadService, the single HistoryRangeSource,
the retrospective service and factories, the Insights and Timeline factories,
portable import/export services, and the root ViewModel factories. The three
factory objects that depend on concrete Activity setup are created as arguments
at the same pre-composition boundary.

Why Activity-owned instead of remember(keys): every required dependency is
already available at the existing post-recovery Activity boundary and has an
Activity/app-stable lifetime. No dependency needs composition-only identity.
Avoiding remember also makes the owner boundary explicit: one graph per
MainActivity instance, fresh graph after Activity recreation.

No process-global graph, static mutable cache, service locator, DI framework,
Composable lambda, NavController, View, Activity, or ActivityResult launcher is
stored by MainFeatureServices. The graph carries repositories, settings seams,
factories, and read-only services only.

WhileSubscribed(5000) interaction

HRTViewModel.currentTimeH remains a StateFlow built with:
  stateIn(scope, started = SharingStarted.WhileSubscribed(5000), initialValue = ...)

When Home's lifecycle-aware collector falls below STARTED, the downstream
collection becomes inactive. The StateFlow upstream is not promised to stop
instantly: its existing WhileSubscribed policy may keep the 1 Hz producer alive
for up to the existing 5000 ms stop timeout.

A-04 does not change that timeout, Clock.millis(), delay(1000), or the source
flow. The device proof waits 6500 ms after STOP and then an additional 1500 ms
to establish the post-timeout quiet period before asserting that no further
clock reads occur.

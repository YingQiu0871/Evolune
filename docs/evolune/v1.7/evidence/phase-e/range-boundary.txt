Range model (contract §8): absolute Instant only; LAST_30_DAYS = capturedAt - 720h,
LAST_90_DAYS = capturedAt - 2160h, both ends inclusive; ALL = occurredAt <= capturedAt
with no lower bound; no DST/calendar/device-zone semantics.

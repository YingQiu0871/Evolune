v1.7.2 Slice D — other-note preservation
========================================

The generic note mechanism (HistoryEntryUiModel.noteRes + HistoryScreen rendering) is
unchanged and still serves every other note kind:

- unrecorded entry: noteRes = history_note_no_recorded_intake,
  secondaryNoteRes = history_note_not_necessarily_missed
  (HistoryPresentationTest `unrecorded entry keeps the non-blaming wording`)
- unmatched entry: noteRes = history_note_plan_unavailable
- matched/unmatched with a derived display date: secondaryNoteRes =
  history_note_current_zone_date
- wording guards unchanged: no remaining history_note_* may claim a legacy origin; the
  neutral caveat keeps its disclaimer phrasing (HistoryPresentationWordingTest)

No global note suppression was introduced; only the single inferred-match branch changed.
Device: HistoryScreenTest still exercises all three entry kinds with their notes.

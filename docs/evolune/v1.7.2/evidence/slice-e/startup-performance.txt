v1.7.2 Slice E (resumed) — startup / performance sanity
=======================================================

Observed during the physical acceptance:
  - cold launches: baseline installs, candidate upgrade, force-stop/relaunch cycles
    and instrumentation runs — the app always reached a usable screen
  - Settings opening, palette switching and History rendering showed no obvious
    stall, ANR or crash loop
  - theme changes applied live without restart; no runaway recomposition or
    persistent high-frequency error logging was observed (see logcat audit)
  - no unexpected background service behavior

No timings are reported because none were measured; no formal benchmark was run.
The frozen device performance suites (PhysicalBackupPerformanceTest,
V15WidgetPerformanceDeviceTest, V15PkPerformanceDeviceTest, V15RoomPerformanceDeviceTest)
remain green in the frozen AVD gate.

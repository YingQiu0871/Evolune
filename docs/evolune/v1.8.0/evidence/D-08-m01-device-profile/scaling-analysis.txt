D-08 scaling analysis

Source-level classification:
  The Home interpolation helper is O(N): two full range scans plus a forward
  scan to the target segment. Chart draw loops also traverse the primary and,
  when present, baseline series. This is the previously known computational
  shape, not a new implementation claim.

Device observations:
  Interpolation wall/CPU timings were sub-millisecond at the 8,641-point
  production size and remained below 1.2 ms p95 wall and 1.1 ms p95 CPU for
  the 1,000-point fixture. The 17,281-point synthetic fixture remained below
  0.6 ms p95 wall and 0.5 ms p95 CPU in the measured run. The three points are
  noisy and do not justify an asymptotic slope from device timing alone.

  Chart warm UI CPU means were 27.7108 ms (1,000), 26.0153 ms (8,641), and
  21.4488 ms (17,281). Warm wall means were 69.8522, 73.4755 and 68.6965 ms.
  The lack of monotonic growth and the stable draw histogram show that these
  values are dominated by fixed Compose/test-frame overhead rather than a
  demonstrated super-linear production failure.

Classification:
  Source work is linear scan work. The device-level chart timing is dominated
  by fixed harness/frame overhead and cannot be reliably classified as a
  production scaling failure from these three points. The only normal product
  size, 8,641 points, shows no credible frame-budget escalation.

EVOLUNE v1.8.0 D-04 timing model

All durations are differences of monotonic System.nanoTime() readings and are recorded in ns.

T0 = generation accepted and measured work begins.
T1 = incumbent enters the complete engine invocation.
T2 = supersession is issued while the incumbent engine invocation is in flight.
T3 = incumbent engine invocation returns.
T4 = first legal observed post-engine boundary rejects/cancels/supersedes the old generation and
     prevents it from publishing.
T5 = successor/current generation publishes its accepted final result.

Required ordering for every incumbent: T0 < T1 < T2 < T3. Retrospective additionally records
T3 <= T4. Home's existing assignment boundary has no independent rejection callback when the
incumbent returns normally; its suppression duration and post-engine suppression overhead are
therefore recorded as the single frozen textual sentinel `NOT_OBSERVABLE`. The sentinel is not a
zero and is not a failed measurement.

The exact per-row values are in raw-measurements.csv. The repeated G0->G1->G2->G3 traces, including
publication classifications, are in repeated-supersession.txt.

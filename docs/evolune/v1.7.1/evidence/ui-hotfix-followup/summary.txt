v1.7.1 History calendar alignment follow-up — summary
=====================================================

Defect (reported from a physical-device screenshot): the selected day number rendered in the
upper part of its rounded selection background instead of being vertically centered.

Root cause (verified by measurement, not assumption)
  HistoryScreen.kt / HistoryCalendarCell was a Column whose content is [day-number Text,
  DayIndicators (fixed 8dp strip)] centered vertically as ONE block. Because the indicator
  strip always reserves 8dp below the number, the text's layout center sat exactly 4dp ABOVE
  the container's center — which is the center of the selection background (clip + background
  are applied to the same container).
  Instrumented measurement before the fix (emulator Pixel_7 AVD, selected day 18):
    fontScale 1.0: number center (262.5, 186.5) vs background center (262.5, 190.5) -> ΔY 4.0dp
    fontScale 2.0: number center (262.7, 232.4) vs background center (262.5, 236.4) -> ΔY 4.0dp
  The horizontal axis was already exact (ΔX 0.0-0.2dp); cell size was 52.57 x 28.19dp.

Correction (proper layout geometry, no nudge)
  The day cell now reserves the SAME 8dp strip ABOVE the number as a symmetric strut
  (`DAY_INDICATOR_STRIP_HEIGHT`, shared by the strut and DayIndicators), keeping the
  selection background and the number in one centering container:
      Column(cell) { Spacer(8dp); Text(number); DayIndicators(8dp) }
  -> number center == background center on BOTH axes, structurally.
  No offset()/translationY/negative padding/magic baseline compensation was used; the
  typography is untouched (no fontSize/lineHeight/platform-font-padding change).
  The strut/indicator strip is structural, font-scale-safe (fixed 8dp reserve) and applies to
  every cell uniformly (selected, today, disabled, empty months).

Geometry after the fix (asserted by the new test)
  - number center == background center within 1.5dp on X and Y at fontScale 1.0 and 2.0;
  - number bounds stay inside the background bounds (no clipping) at both scales;
  - neighboring cells (17 / 18 / 19) share the same row top/bottom within 0.5dp;
  - the selection remains visible and clickable (content description carries 已选择);
  - the touch target did not shrink: width unchanged (~52.6dp), height 28.19dp -> 36.19dp
    (+8dp from the symmetric strut; a growth, never a reduction).
  Note: the calendar row pitch grows uniformly by 8dp; rows stay aligned and the calendar
  remains scrollable with no clipping (confirmed on the physical device).

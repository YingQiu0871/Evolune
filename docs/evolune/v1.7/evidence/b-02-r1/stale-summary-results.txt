# Stale responses with discriminating summaries

## Frozen rule
A superseded success, ordinary failure or contract violation must not replace the newer state;
the surviving **summary** must be the newer request's, not only its selection/endpoints.

## Tests that pin it
- `a stale success that ignores cancellation cannot replace the newer selection`
- `a stale failure cannot turn the newer success into an error`
- `a stale contract violation cannot replace the newer content`

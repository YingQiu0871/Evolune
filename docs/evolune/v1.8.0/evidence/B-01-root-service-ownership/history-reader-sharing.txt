EVOLUNE v1.8.0 B-01 — HISTORYREADSERVICE SHARING

MainFeatureServices creates exactly one HistoryReadService from the two
repository dependencies.

The same object is wired to:
- HistoryViewModelFactory.historyReadService
- InsightsViewModelFactory.historyReadService
- RetrospectivePkService.history
- RetrospectivePkViewModelFactory.allAvailableHistorySource

One HistoryRangeSource closure over that reader is wired to:
- RetrospectivePkViewModelFactory.historyRangeSource
- TimelineViewModelFactory.rangeSource

MainFeatureServicesTest directly asserts these identities through the test-only
JVM inspection seam. No consumer intentionally requires a distinct history
reader, and no feature semantics were changed.

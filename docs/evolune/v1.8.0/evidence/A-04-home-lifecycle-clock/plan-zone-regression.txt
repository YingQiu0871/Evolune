Plan and timezone change while stopped

No plan or timezone identity logic was changed. The A-03 identity and invalidation
guards remain in ScheduleBoundaryTrackerTest:
  plan change invalidates old boundary without synthetic crossing
  timezone change invalidates boundary without crossing old zone
  empty plans clear pending boundary and later plans initialize fresh

Because A-04 does not reset the tracker on lifecycle stop/start, the first resumed
observation still runs through the existing identity-first logic. A changed plan
snapshot or ZoneId therefore invalidates stale retained state before any crossing
can be reported.

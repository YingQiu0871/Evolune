EVOLUNE v1.8.0 A-01 — DISPATCHER DESIGN

Boundary: RetrospectivePkService. Its public estimate(request) is the
computation seam and wraps the existing service orchestration in
withContext(computationDispatcher).

Dispatcher policy:
- Default production dispatcher: Dispatchers.Default.
- Internal constructor accepts an injected CoroutineDispatcher for deterministic
  ownership tests.
- The existing public composition-root constructor remains
  RetrospectivePkService(historyReadService).

The whole service computation stays together: validation, the existing
history read, extraction/projection, step calculation, curve-runner call, and
series validation execute in the computation context. The curve runner and
SimulationEngine therefore no longer execute on the caller/Main context.

The coordinator's three reads remain in their existing order and remain
sequential/non-atomic. ViewModel generation checks and publication remain in
the existing caller-owned ViewModel flow. Cancellation is not caught or
converted by the dispatcher boundary; structured cancellation propagates
through withContext.

No new global scope, long-lived scope, parallel read, cache, numerical rewrite,
or SimulationEngine change was introduced.

Home collection path before A-04

Source:
  app/src/main/java/io/github/yingqiu0871/evolune/ui/screens/HomeScreen.kt

HomeScreen collected:
  pkState with collectAsState()
  doseTimePoints with collectAsState()
  enabledPlans with collectAsState()
  currentTimeH with collectAsState()

The currentTimeH value was passed to HomeScreenContent as a State<Double>. The
composition retained a LaunchedEffect(enabledPlans) whose snapshotFlow read that
state and called the ViewModel boundary observer on clock changes. Plain
collectAsState() did not impose a lifecycle STARTED threshold, so a retained Home
composition could remain subscribed while its Activity lifecycle was STOPPED.

HRTViewModel.currentTimeH already used:
  clock.millis() / MILLIS_PER_HOUR
  delay(1000)
  SharingStarted.WhileSubscribed(5000)

No source change was made to HRTViewModel.kt or ScheduleBoundaryTracker.kt.

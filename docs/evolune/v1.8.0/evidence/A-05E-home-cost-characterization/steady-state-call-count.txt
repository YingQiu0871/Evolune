Steady-state call-count probe

Probe conditions:
  Fixed UTC zone; one enabled daily plan with one 08:00 slot; provider body was
  the same seven-day domain predictor used by Home's nextPlanForkPointTimeH.
  Ticks were deterministic one-second observations.

Scenario                         Observations  Provider calls  Results used  Crossings  Redundant calls
steady_60 (pre-boundary)                 60             60             1          0              59
crossing_600 (one exact crossing)       600            600             2          1             598

The first scenario establishes the stable retained-B case. The second begins
before the daily boundary and includes the exact crossing at the final tick;
the second accepted result is the following day's strict-future boundary.

The minimum provider-call count under the current A-03 semantics is one call at
initialization plus one call per consumed boundary. Therefore the minimum for
the two scenarios is respectively 1 and 2, versus the observed 60 and 600.
This is a structural redundancy result, not a wall-clock or battery claim.

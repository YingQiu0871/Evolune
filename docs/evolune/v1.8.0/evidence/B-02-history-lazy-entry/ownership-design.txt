B-02 ownership after

MainFeatureServices remains created once in MainActivity.onCreate and captured by setContent.
HistoryReadService and HistoryRangeSource remain passive Activity-owned objects.

MainActivity now passes the stable historyViewModelFactory as data to AppNavigation. The History
destination is the narrow creation boundary:
  composable(Screen.HISTORY.route) { entry ->
      val owner = activity as? ViewModelStoreOwner ?: entry
      val historyViewModel = viewModel(viewModelStoreOwner = owner, factory = historyViewModelFactory)
      HistoryScreen(viewModel = historyViewModel, ...)
  }

The Activity owner matches the established Insights, Retrospective, and Timeline ownership idiom.
The existing History route and navigation actions are unchanged. No DI framework, static/global
cache, composable service construction, or route change was introduced.

The production root supplies the one MainFeatureServices.historyRangeSource to the
HistoryViewModelFactory constructor. The direct Android saved-state factory test also supplies an
explicit HistoryRangeSource seam; no factory constructor creates a hidden production reader seam.

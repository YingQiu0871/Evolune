Cancellation proof

SettingsUpdateCheckTest.cancellationPropagatesWithoutPublishingFailure passed.

The test injects a deterministic CancellationException into the launched
update-check coroutine and verifies:
  - the update fetch was invoked;
  - the result remains Checking rather than becoming Error;
  - the owning SupervisorJob remains active, so cancellation did not become an
    ordinary failure or cancel the owner.

The production path catches CancellationException before Exception and rethrows
immediately. No logger callback or failure-state mutation runs on cancellation.
